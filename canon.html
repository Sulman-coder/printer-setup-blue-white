Basic setup checklist

  1. 01
    Prepare your printer

    Connect the power cable, turn the printer on, and follow any setup prompts on its screen.

  2. 02
    Connect your device

    Use USB or connect the printer and computer to the same Wi-Fi network, as supported by your model.

  3. 03
    Find your exact model

    Check the label on the printer so you can select the correct model on the official support site.

  4. 04
    Install and test

    Follow the manufacturer's instructions, then print a test page to confirm everything works.
